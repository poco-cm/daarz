## Decision 1 — The third institution is `memory`, not `population` or `observability`

Order 2 recommended `factory`, `safety`, and `memory`, and that recommendation was
followed. Recording why the alternative was rejected, since `population/` and
`observability/` already have working code from phase 3 and were the obvious candidates:

`population` and `observability` **describe** the civilization. `memory` **changes** it.
A third institution chosen from the describing kind would have made Formation a
reorganisation of what phase 3 already built. Choosing memory forced a real new
capability — state that outlives a run — and produced a falsifiable test.

Consequence to state plainly: `population/` and `observability/` now contain running code
but are **not** activated institutions. They have no protocol address, and no other
component may address them over `protocols/`. They are internal capabilities of the
runtime, not parties to the civil order. This is a real inconsistency in kind, accepted
because Order 2 fixed the count at exactly three and raising it to five would exceed
authority.

## Decision 2 — Activation is a single fact with a single owner

`institutions/runtime/activation.py` holds the activated set. Nothing else keeps its own
opinion about which institutions are live. The alternative — each institution asserting
its own status — makes disagreement possible, and a civilization that disagrees about
which of its institutions exist has no reliable answer to any other question.

## Decision 3 — Dormant institutions are refused at construction, not at delivery

`InstitutionRequest.__post_init__` validates sender and recipient. An invalid message
therefore cannot be constructed, held, logged, or half-sent. The alternative — refusing at
delivery — permits invalid messages to exist and be passed around.

## Decision 4 — Every action has exactly one owning institution

`ACTION_OWNER` maps each action to the one institution that serves it. Sending `remember`
to `safety` is refused even though both are activated. Actions are not capabilities that
several institutions might happen to implement.

## Decision 5 — `halt` is requestable over the protocol; release is not expressible

Stated in full in the milestone. The asymmetry is deliberate and constitutional.

## Decision 6 — The snapshot is generated, not written

`archive/institutional/SNAPSHOT-2026-08-09-formation.md` is produced by parsing the
source of the activated institutions with `ast`. A hand-written snapshot records what
someone believed was true. This one records what the code actually contained.
